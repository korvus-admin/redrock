The demo takes 10–20 seconds to boot. Nothing is saved when you close the tab, so feel free to experiment. Demo login is admin / password.

What you're looking at

A WordPress block theme plus one small plugin. All 12 services, 28 providers and the patient reviews from the current site are already loaded. No Elementor, no OceanWP, no jQuery.

Editing on the fly

Every section is editable in the WordPress editor, but the layouts are locked. You can change any headline, paragraph, photo or button without being able to break the design. Phone, hours and address live in one settings screen and update everywhere.

Adding content

New surgeons, services and testimonials are simple forms in the dashboard. The homepage and listing pages update automatically.

Going live is an update, not a rebuild

  1. Upload the plugin zip and activate it.
  2. Upload the theme zip and preview it on the existing site.
  3. Publish when ready. Existing plugins, forms, URLs and payments keep working.
